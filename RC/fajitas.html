Fajita Recipe

Ingredients

  • Chicken Thighs
  • Onion
  • Bell Peppers
  • Spice Rub
    Salt
    Pepper
    Cumin
    Paprika
    Garlic powder
    Onion powder
    Oregano
    Basil
    Lemon juice
    Chilli Extract
    Red Pepper
  • Wraps
  • Guacamole
  • Salsa

    • Method

    1. Finely chop onions and put in pot on low heat
    2. Prepare chicken thighs and put in bowl with spice rub
    3. Use hands to ensure the chicken is evenly coated
    4. Slice the bell pepper and place in pot with onion
    5. Heat a non-stick metal pan and put the chicken in
    6. When the chicken is seared on both sides add to the pot
    7. Cover wraps in foil and add to an oven preheated to 180 degrees celsius for 8 minutes
    8. Serve how desired
